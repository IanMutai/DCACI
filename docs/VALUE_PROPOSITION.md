# Value Proposition

## National Climate Transparency Platform (NCTP)
### Transforming Climate Transparency for Developing Countries

---

## Executive Summary

The National Climate Transparency Platform (NCTP) is a comprehensive, integrated solution that enables countries to meet their Paris Agreement obligations while unlocking economic opportunities through carbon markets. By combining MRV (Monitoring, Reporting, Verification), NDC Tools, and a Carbon Registry into a single platform, NCTP eliminates the fragmentation that plagues current climate data systems.

**Bottom Line:** NCTP helps countries comply with international requirements, track progress toward climate goals, and generate revenue—all from one integrated platform.
