# Government User Guide

## National Climate Transparency Platform for Climate Ministries and Agencies

---

## Introduction

Welcome to the National Climate Transparency Platform (NCTP). This guide is designed for government officials in climate ministries, environment agencies, and related departments who will be using the platform to manage their country's climate transparency obligations.

---

## What is NCTP?

NCTP is an integrated platform that combines three essential climate systems:

| System | What It Does | Your Role |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| **MRV System** | Tracks national greenhouse gas emissions | Coordinate data collection, review inventory, submit reports |
| **NDC Tools** | Manages your country's climate targets | Set targets, track progress, plan policies |
| **Carbon Registry** | Manages carbon projects and credits | Approve projects, oversee transactions, authorize ITMOs |

Together, these systems help your country meet its Paris Agreement obligations while potentially generating revenue through carbon markets.

## Understanding the MRV System

### What is MRV?
MRV stands for Monitoring, Reporting, and Verification. It's how your country tracks its greenhouse gas emissions across all sectors.

### The Five IPCC Sectors

| Sector | What It Covers | Key Data Sources |
|--------|----------------|------------------|
| **Energy** | Power plants, transport, industry fuel use | Energy ministry, utilities |
| **Industry (IPPU)** | Manufacturing processes, F-gases | Industry ministry, factories |
| **Agriculture** | Livestock, crops, fertilizers | Agriculture ministry, census |
| **Forestry (LULUCF)** | Forests, land use changes | Forestry department, satellite data |
| **Waste** | Landfills, wastewater | Municipal authorities |

### Annual Inventory Cycle

```
January-March:    Collect activity data from sources
April-June:       Calculate emissions, compile inventory
July-September:   Quality assurance, internal review
October-December: Finalize, prepare reports, plan improvements
```

### Key Actions in MRV

**Reviewing Sector Data**
1. Navigate to MRV → Sector Inventories
2. Select the sector and year
3. Review activity data entries
4. Check emission calculations
5. Flag any issues for follow-up

**Approving the Inventory**
1. Navigate to MRV → Annual Inventory
2. Review completion status
3. Check QA/QC completion
4. Approve for submission

## Using NDC Tools

### Understanding Your NDC

Your Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC) is your country's climate commitment under the Paris Agreement. The NDC Tools help you:

- Track progress toward your targets
- Plan policies to achieve your goals
- Report on implementation

### Types of NDC Targets

| Target Type | Example | How Progress is Measured |
|-------------|---------|--------------------------|
| Absolute | -30% below 2010 by 2030 | Compare current to base year |
| BAU | -25% below business-as-usual | Compare current to projected |
| Intensity | -40% emissions/GDP | Calculate emission intensity |
| Net-zero | Carbon neutral by 2050 | Track gross emissions + removals |

### Viewing Progress

1. Navigate to NDC Tools → Progress Dashboard
2. See overall target achievement
3. View sector-by-sector breakdown
4. Identify gaps and priorities

### Policy Tracking

Track which policies are contributing to your NDC:
1. Navigate to NDC Tools → Policies
2. See status: Planned → Adopted → Implemented
3. View expected vs. actual emission reductions
4. Identify policies needing acceleration

## Managing the Carbon Registry

### Carbon Market Opportunities

Your country can participate in carbon markets through:

| Market Type | How It Works | Benefits |
|-------------|--------------|----------|
| **Voluntary Carbon Market (VCM)** | Private buyers purchase credits | Revenue for project developers |
| **Article 6.2** | Country-to-country ITMO transfers | Revenue for government, NDC flexibility |

### Project Lifecycle Overview

```
1. PCN (Concept Note)    → Initial idea, eligibility check
2. PDD (Design Document) → Detailed plan, methodology
3. Authorization         → Government approval
4. Implementation        → Project construction
5. Monitoring            → Annual performance tracking
6. Issuance              → Credits generated
7. Transfer/Retirement   → Credits sold or used
```

### Your Role in Project Approval

**For VCM Projects:**
- Review for national compliance
- Issue Letter of No Objection
- Monitor community benefits

**For Article 6.2 Projects:**
- Verify alignment with NDC
- Check carbon budget availability
- Issue Letter of Authorization
- Approve corresponding adjustments

### Article 6.2 Authorization Process

When authorizing ITMOs for international transfer:

1. **Check Eligibility**
   - Is the project registered and verified?
   - Is the acquiring country identified?
   - Is there a bilateral agreement?

2. **Check Carbon Budget**
   - How many ITMOs are requested?
   - What's available under the budget?
   - What's the impact on NDC achievement?

3. **Approve Corresponding Adjustment**
   - Understand that transferred ITMOs add to your reported emissions
   - Ensure this is acceptable for NDC compliance

4. **Issue Authorization**
   - Sign the Letter of Authorization
   - Notify the acquiring country
   - Update CAD records

## Generating Reports

### Types of Reports

| Report | Purpose | Frequency | Deadline |
|--------|---------|-----------|----------|
| **BTR** | Biennial Transparency Report to UNFCCC | Every 2 years | Per UNFCCC schedule |
| **NIR** | National Inventory Report | With BTR | Per UNFCCC schedule |
| **Annual Report** | Internal government use | Yearly | December |
| **Registry Report** | Carbon market activity | Quarterly | End of quarter |

### Generating a BTR

1. Navigate to Reports → BTR Generator
2. Select reporting period
3. Review data pulled from each system:
   - MRV: Inventory data for Chapter 1
   - NDC Tools: Progress data for Chapter 2
   - Registry: ITMO data for Article 6 section
4. Review and approve each chapter
5. Generate final document
6. Submit to UNFCCC

## Data Quality and Review

### Quality Indicators

The platform shows quality status using:

- ✅ **Green**: Complete, verified data
- ⚠️ **Yellow**: Data available, needs review
- ❌ **Red**: Missing or problematic data

### When You See Issues

1. Click on the flagged item
2. Review the specific issue
3. Contact the responsible data provider
4. Update or approve with documentation
5. Log the resolution

### Preparing for International Review

Under the Enhanced Transparency Framework, your BTR will be reviewed by international experts. The platform helps you:

- Document all methodologies clearly
- Maintain audit trails
- Track improvements from previous reviews
- Prepare response materials
